Washing Daddy customer app and WD Washer app

You can ask us to delete your account and all data linked to it at any time.

How to request deletion

  • Email hello@washingdaddy.com from any address, with the subject "Delete my Washing Daddy account", and include the phone number you sign in with.
  • Or message us on the number your washer or the Washing Daddy team gave you, from the phone number on the account.

What gets deleted

  • Your sign-in (phone number), name, area, car details and parking spot.
  • Your wash history, ratings, requests and wash photos.

We complete deletion within 30 days. We may keep payment and invoice records for as long as the law requires; these are held by our payment partner and in our accounts, and are not used for anything else.
