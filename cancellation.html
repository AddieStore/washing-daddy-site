Cancellation and Refund Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

This Cancellation and Refund Policy (the “Policy”) is issued by Washing Daddy Private Limited (CIN: U45200DC2026PTC474412), a private limited company incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013, having its registered office at GH-6/274, Prachin Apartments, Paschim Vihar, New Delhi – 110087 (the “Company”, “Washing Daddy”, “we” or “us”, which expression includes its successors and permitted assigns). The Company operates a technology platform, comprising the website https://washingdaddy.com/ and the Washing Daddy and WD Partner apps (the “Platform”), through which customers (“Users” or “you”) book doorstep car washing and car care services (“Wash Services”) that are performed by independent washers (“Washers”). This Policy forms part of our Terms & Conditions at https://washingdaddy.com/terms.html (the “Terms”), and capitalised terms not defined here have the meaning given in the Terms.

1. Services Offered on the Platform

The Platform offers the following, and each has its own cancellation and refund rules:

(A) Instant Services – Wash Services that a Washer provides immediately or within a short period (typically within 45 to 60 minutes) of booking.

(B) Scheduled Services – Wash Services booked for a later date and time slot.

(C) Recurring Services, Passes and Monthly Plans – Wash Services booked to repeat (for example weekly or on alternate days), WD Pass visits, and monthly plans such as WD Standard, WD Pro, WD Exclusive and WD Reserve.

2. Cancellation

A) Instant Services

(a) You may cancel an Instant Service free of charge until a Washer has been assigned. You will be refunded in full.

(b) Once a Washer has been assigned but has not yet set off, you may still cancel, and you will be refunded in full where the Washer has not yet started travelling to you.

(c) If you cancel after the Washer has set off for your location, a cancellation fee of up to ₹50 may apply, and the balance will be refunded.

(d) Once the Washer has reached your car or started the Wash Service, the booking cannot be cancelled, and it is treated as completed.

B) Scheduled Services

(a) Cancel up to two (2) hours before the start of the booked slot: full refund, with no charge.

(b) Cancel less than two (2) hours before the slot, but before the Washer has set off for your location: we will normally refund you in full.

(c) Cancel after the Washer has set off, or the Washer reaches your car and cannot do the wash because of a reason on your side (for example the car is not at the location, access is denied, or you cannot be reached): a cancellation fee of up to ₹50 applies and the balance (if any) is refunded. If the Washer had already started the work, the booking is treated as completed.

(d) If the Washer is unavailable or does not arrive: full refund, or a free reschedule at your choice.

C) Bookings made with a WD Pass visit

(a) Cancel more than two (2) hours before the slot: the visit is returned to your WD Pass.

(b) Cancel less than two (2) hours before the slot, or in case of a customer no-show: the visit is treated as used and is not returned. If the Washer had not yet set off, we will return the visit as a goodwill gesture at our discretion.

(c) If the Washer is unavailable or does not arrive: the visit is returned to your pass, or a free reschedule is given.

(d) Unused visits expire with the pass (currently thirty (30) days from the date of purchase) and are not refunded.

D) Recurring Services and Monthly Plans

Cancelling a single visit: you may cancel a single visit within a recurring booking or plan free of charge if you do so at least two (2) hours before the scheduled start. If you cancel later than that and the Washer has set off, the cancellation fee in Section 2B may apply; if you are not available when the Washer arrives, the visit is counted as used.

Cancelling the whole plan: you may stop a recurring booking or monthly plan at any time. A plan continues until the end of the period you have already paid for. We do not refund the part of a paid period that has passed or the remainder of a paid period if you cancel mid-way, except where the law requires or where we have failed to deliver the Wash Services. Visits scheduled within two (2) hours of your cancelling stay billable.

Pausing: some plans allow you to pause visits from the app. As at the date of this Policy, customers on WD Pro or a higher plan may pause for up to seven (7) days in a calendar month. Paused days are not available for refund. Pausing is not available on other plans.

3. Cancellations by Washers or the Company

3.1. If a Washer or the Company cancels your booking, or we cannot find a Washer for your booking, you will receive a full refund of the amount paid for that booking. You may instead choose to reschedule the Wash Service free of charge or to receive the amount as WD Money.

3.2. If bad weather (such as heavy rain), a water shortage, a building or society restriction, or a force majeure event (see Section 9) prevents a visit, we will reschedule the visit or refund or credit the amount for that visit, and no cancellation fee will apply.

4. Refunds

4.1. Instant Services. Refunds are given if you cancel as described in Section 2A, or if: the Washer did not arrive; the Wash Service that was done was different from what you booked; or the Wash Service could not be completed for reasons on the part of the Washer.

4.2. Scheduled Services. Refunds are given as described in Section 2B and 2C.

4.3. Recurring Services and Monthly Plans. Refunds are given as described in Section 2D, and for visits the Company fails to deliver.

4.4. How refunds are paid. Refunds are made as follows:

(a) to WD Money, instantly, if the booking was paid from WD Money or if you choose a refund to WD Money; or

(b) to your original payment method (for example UPI, card or net banking), within five (5) to seven (7) working days after the refund is initiated, depending on your bank or payment provider.

4.5. WD Money, wallet and promotional credits. Refunds of amounts paid with promotional credits are returned as promotional credits. Promotional credits keep their original validity (fifteen (15) days from the date originally credited), and cash balance remains valid for one (1) year from the date it was credited, as set out in the Terms. Coupon discounts are not refunded in cash; if a booking made with a coupon is cancelled in line with this Policy, the coupon may be restored where it is still valid and its terms allow.

4.6. Where money was debited but the booking was not confirmed, the amount is returned to the original payment method automatically, typically within five (5) to seven (7) working days.

5. Quality Issues and Damage

5.1. If you are not satisfied with a Wash Service, please report the issue through the app or at Business@washingdaddy.com within twenty-four (24) hours of the visit, with photographs where possible. After internal review of the Washer’s before-and-after photographs and records, we may, depending on the case:

(a) arrange a free re-wash;

(b) give you a partial or full refund; or

(c) give you the equivalent amount as service credits (WD Money).

5.2. If you believe a Washer has damaged your vehicle, please follow the procedure in the Terms (Clauses 6.11 and 11.3), including reporting within twenty-four (24) hours with photographs. Quality and damage matters are decided case by case. Washing removes dirt and dust only and does not repair existing paint, scratches or stains.

6. Refund Processing Timeline

6.1. Refunds to WD Money are credited instantly. Refunds to your original payment method are initiated within two (2) working days of approval and reach your account within five (5) to seven (7) working days of initiation, subject to your bank or payment provider.

6.2. You can see the status of your refund in the app, under Refunds. If you have not received a refund after seven (7) working days, please write to us with your booking details and we will follow it up.

7. Notice for Refunds and Cancellations

Any issue about a refund or cancellation must be raised within forty-eight (48) hours of the Wash Service (or of the time it was scheduled), through the app or at Business@washingdaddy.com. This applies to Instant, Scheduled and Recurring Services. Quality and damage issues must be reported within twenty-four (24) hours, as set out in Section 5.

8. Misuse of this Policy

The Company may refuse a refund, or limit your use of the Platform, where it reasonably believes there are repeated cancellations, no-shows, abuse of the cancellation or refund process, or fraud. Washers may also refuse to continue where access is not given or a visit is unsafe.

9. Force Majeure

The Company is not liable for delays or cancellations beyond its reasonable control. These events include:

  • acts of God, heavy rain, flooding, extreme heat or other natural events;
  • water shortages or restrictions on water use;
  • war, terrorism, riots or civil commotion;
  • epidemics or pandemics;
  • strikes or labour disputes;
  • governmental actions, orders or restrictions;
  • failure of utilities or telecommunications networks; or
  • any other event that is unforeseeable and beyond the reasonable control of the affected party.

Where a visit cannot be performed because of such an event, we will reschedule it or refund or credit the amount for that visit.
