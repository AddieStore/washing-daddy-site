Welcome to the privacy policy of Washing Daddy (“Privacy Policy” or “Policy”).

Washing Daddy Private Limited (CIN: U45200DC2026PTC474412), having its registered office at GH-6/274, Prachin Apartments, Paschim Vihar, New Delhi – 110087, and its affiliates (together, “Washing Daddy”, “we” or “us”) provide a doorstep car wash service and the technology that connects customers who want their cars washed with washers who perform the wash. This Policy explains how we collect, store, use, process and disclose personal data when you access or use our website at https://washingdaddy.com/, the Washing Daddy customer app, or the WD Partner app for washers (together, the “Platform”), or use the products and services we offer on or through the Platform (the “Services”).

Unless defined in this Policy, capitalised terms have the meaning given in our Terms & Conditions, available at https://washingdaddy.com/terms.html (“Terms”). By using the Services, you confirm that you have read and agree to this Policy and consent to the processing described in it. If you do not agree, please do not use the Services.

1. Background and Key Information

1.1. This Policy applies to customers who use the customer app and website, and to washers who use the WD Partner app. Where a section applies to only one group, we say so.

1.2. In short: we collect what we need to sign you in, find your car, send a washer, record that the wash was done, take payment and support you. We do not sell your personal data. We do not use advertising networks or third-party analytics or advertising SDKs in our apps.

2. Personal Data That We Collect

2.1. We collect different types of personal data about you. This includes:

Contact Data: your mobile number, your name, your area or society, house or flat details and, if you give it, your email address.

Identity and Profile Data: your name and, for washers, identity documents and records (such as ID proof and kit records) that you give us when you are onboarded.

Vehicle Data: your car’s make, model, registration number, colour or size, parking spot and parking instructions, and, if you choose to add it, a photograph of your car from your phone gallery.

Service and Photo Data: photographs taken by the washer of your car before and after the wash, wash records (date, time, status, duration, services done, water used), your ratings and feedback, notes and requests you send us (for example rewash, skip or pause), and attendance, round and work records of washers.

Transaction Data: details of the Services you have booked, passes, plans, coupons, referrals, WD Money balance and ledger, refunds, and payment references and status provided to us by payment processors. We do not receive or store your full card number, CVV or UPI PIN.

Communications Data: your communication preferences, the messages and calls you exchange with us, and records of support requests.

Location Data: the address and parking location you give us. For washers, the WD Partner app may use the phone’s location while a round is running to confirm arrival and work done, only after the washer allows location permission. The customer app does not track your phone’s location.

Technical Data: limited technical information that is generated when you use the Platform, such as server logs, time stamps, the type of app version and your sign-in identifier. We do not collect device advertising IDs.

2.2. We also use aggregated or anonymised data, such as statistics on bookings, for any purpose. Aggregated data is not personal data as it does not reveal your identity. If we combine it with personal data so that it can identify you, we treat it as personal data under this Policy.

2.3. What happens if I refuse to provide my personal data? Where we need personal data by law, or to perform the contract with you (for example to sign you in, find your car or take payment), and you do not provide it, we may not be able to provide the Services, and may have to cancel or limit your access.

3. How Do We Collect Personal Data?

3.1. Direct interactions. You give us personal data when you create an account or profile, add or edit your car and address, book or pay for a Service, enter a coupon or referral, rate a wash, report a problem, give feedback or contact us.

3.2. Automated means. When you use the Platform we automatically generate and receive limited technical data, such as logs and time stamps, and the app and website store small items of data on your device (such as your sign-in session and preferences) so that they work properly. These are essential to the working of the Platform; we do not use advertising cookies or tracking pixels.

3.3. Third parties and others. We receive data from: your assigned washer (such as confirmation of the wash and photographs); payment processors (payment status and references); the person who referred you, where you use a referral link; and, for washers, the references and documents provided during onboarding.

4. How Do We Use Your Personal Data?

4.1. We use your personal data only when the law allows us to, mostly to provide the Services or to comply with the law. We use it:

(a) to verify your identity, sign you in by one-time password (OTP) and create and manage your account;

(b) to provide the Services, to match you with a washer, to show live wash status and to record that the wash was done, including through before-and-after photographs;

(c) to enable the washer to find your car and perform the Wash Services, and to calculate and display the washer’s earnings;

(d) to track transactions, process payments, give refunds and manage WD Money, passes, plans, coupons and referral rewards;

(e) to send you booking, arrival, payment and service messages by SMS, WhatsApp, phone, email and in-app notifications;

(f) to respond to your support requests and complaints, and to investigate damage claims and disputes;

(g) to improve the Services, our service quality and our business, and to monitor service standards (including ratings);

(h) to send you updates and offers about the Services, which you can opt out of;

(i) to administer and protect our business and the Platform, including troubleshooting, security, fraud prevention and internal operations;

(j) to enforce our Terms, comply with legal obligations, respond to court orders, and establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

4.2. By using the Services and creating an account, you authorise us, our washers and our service providers to contact you by phone, SMS, WhatsApp, email or otherwise in connection with the Services.

4.3. Information about you, whether or not you give it to us directly (including your correspondence with us), may be collected, compiled and shared by us to provide the Services to you, with washers, service providers (such as hosting, messaging and payment providers), consultants, lawyers and auditors, and other entities in the Washing Daddy group.

4.4. We may disclose data without your consent where required by law or by a court or government agency. We do this in good faith where reasonably necessary to enforce this Policy or the Terms, or to comply with applicable laws.

5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

5.1. Cookies and similar technologies (such as local storage) are small items of data that a site or app stores on your device so that it can recognise your device and remember information.

5.2. Our website and apps use these only for essential purposes: to keep you signed in, to remember your preferences and to make the Platform work. We do not use them for advertising.

5.3. If you open links to third-party sites (for example a payment page or social media), those sites may use their own cookies, which we do not control. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, but parts of the Platform may then not work.

6. Disclosures of Your Personal Data

6.1. We may share your personal data with the parties below for the purposes set out in Section 4:

(a) Washers, to enable them to provide the Wash Services (for example your name, area, car details, parking spot and booking details). Washers see only the data they need for their own rounds.

(b) Other companies in the Washing Daddy group.

(c) Service providers who work on our behalf, including: Google Firebase (Authentication, which sends the OTP by SMS, and Cloud Firestore, which stores your account and wash data); payment processors such as Cashfree and Razorpay; our automation and hosting provider (an n8n workflow server we operate); messaging providers (SMS, WhatsApp, Telegram, which we use internally to notify our team of photos, payments and operational alerts); and cloud-based tools we use to keep our customer records (for example Notion).

(d) Other registered users, where you ask us to or explicitly consent (for example when you use a referral).

(e) Regulators, courts and other bodies, where required by law or regulation.

6.2. We do not sell or rent your personal data. We require all third parties to respect the security of your personal data and to treat it in accordance with the law. We do not allow them to use your personal data for their own purposes, and only permit them to process it for specified purposes and in accordance with our instructions.

You may ask for deletion of your data (see Section 8), withdraw your consent to processing (which may mean we cannot provide the Services), and, as the law permits, nominate another person to exercise your rights in the event of your death or incapacity.

8.1. In the app: open the Washing Daddy customer app, go to Profile, then Legal & Privacy, then Delete account, and follow the steps. Before you confirm, the app shows what will happen: upcoming bookings will be cancelled, pass and WD Money balances will be lost, and your profile, addresses and history will no longer be available.

8.2. By email: write to Business@washingdaddy.com from the registered mobile number or email, or call +91 77018 08924, and ask for your account and data to be deleted. Washers can ask the same way.

8.3. We will process your request and delete your account and personal data within thirty (30) days. Once your account is deleted, you will lose access to all Services.

8.4. 10.1. We use appropriate security measures to protect your personal data from unauthorised access and disclosure. These include: sign-in by OTP; access rules on our database so that each customer can read and write only their own data and washers see only their own rounds; administrator access limited to the business; encrypted connections; and server-side checks on prices and payments.

10.2. Payment details (such as card numbers) are handled by the payment processor and not stored by us.

10.3. You are responsible for keeping your OTP and phone secure. We will never ask you for your OTP. We are not responsible for unauthorised use of your account arising from your sharing your OTP or device. If you think your account has been misused, tell us immediately.

10.4. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. If a personal data breach occurs that affects you, we will notify you and the authorities as the law requires.

11. Data Retention

11.1. We keep your personal data for as long as your account is active and as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this Policy, and for a reasonable period afterwards to meet our legal obligations and handle disputes, claims and complaints.

11.2. When you delete your account, we delete or anonymise your personal data within thirty (30) days, except for records we must keep by law (see Section 8.4).

11.3. We may keep aggregated or anonymised data, which can no longer be linked to you, indefinitely for research or statistics.
